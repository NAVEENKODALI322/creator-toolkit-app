Privacy Policy

Creator Toolkit app. Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Who we are

The Creator Toolkit app ("the app", "we", "our") is created and operated by Naveen Kodali, a graphic designer and YouTube creator from Andhra Pradesh, India. The app is the mobile version of the website creatortoolkits.in.

Contact: naveenkodali322@gmail.com

2. What the app does

The app helps YouTube creators write titles, hooks, tags, hashtags and video descriptions with the help of AI. It also has an upload checklist, and it keeps your past results on your phone.

3. What we collect

  • No account. You do not sign up, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.
  • What you type into a tool. When you tap Generate, the text you typed (for example your video topic, channel name or keywords) is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server. Our server passes it to the AI service (see section 5) to create the result and sends the result back to you.
  • We do not save what you type. We do not keep your inputs or the results in a database and we do not link them to you, except when you choose to send a report (next point). Please do not type private or sensitive information into the tools.
  • Reports. If you tap "Report a problem" under a result, we receive your topic, the result and the reason you chose, and we keep it in our server log so we can improve the results and filters. We receive nothing else with it.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs of requests, such as IP address, time, device type and error messages, for security and to keep the service running.

4. What stays on your phone

Your history of results, your favorites and your checklist ticks are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We cannot see them. You can delete them at any time from About > Clear all saved data, by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Services we use

  • Groq (AI service). It receives the text you typed into a tool, to generate the result.
  • Vercel (hosting). It runs our server and may keep the technical logs described above.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing.

6. Ads and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising ID. It does not use location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other special permission. It only needs an internet connection to generate results.

7. Links to other sites

The About screen has links to our website and to an email address. Those open in your browser or email app. The website has its own Privacy Policy, which covers cookies and advertising on the website.

8. AI results

The results are drafts made by AI. They can be wrong or similar to other people's text. Please check every result before you use it. We do not promise any views, subscribers or income.

9. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your choices

Because we do not keep an account or save your inputs, there is no personal profile for us to show or delete. If you want to ask about data we may hold, such as technical logs, write to us at the email address above and we will reply.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
